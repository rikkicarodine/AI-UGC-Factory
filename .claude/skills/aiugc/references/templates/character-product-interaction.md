# Character Product Interaction Prompt Template (VERBATIM — use word-for-word, no edits)

Take the uploaded character reference image and product reference image(s) and create one hyper-realistic 9:16 character-product interaction image.

Use the character reference as the identity anchor: face, body, skin tone, hairstyle, outfit, accessories, proportions, and overall smartphone realism must stay consistent.

Use the product reference image(s) as the only source for the product. Preserve its visible shape, scale, color, material, logo, label, cap, pump, packaging, texture, reflections, and proportions. Do not invent unseen sides or missing details.

Place the character standing upright, facing the camera directly, against the fixed matte cool slate-gray backdrop, hex #6F7478. The backdrop must be plain, evenly lit, non-reflective, and free of scenery, props, decorations, or text.

The character should naturally interact with the product based on the product type, size, and normal use case. For example, a serum may be held near the face, a purse may be held by the handle or worn naturally, a bottle may be held upright, and a wearable item may be worn or lightly adjusted.

Choose the interaction that makes the product easiest to understand. The product must remain fully visible, clearly recognizable, accurately scaled, and central to the image. Keep important details such as the label, logo, cap, handle, pump, or box front facing the camera whenever possible.

Hands, fingers, clothing, hair, or body position must not cover important product details. If natural use would hide the product, prioritize a clear presentation pose instead.

Maintain realistic product scale relative to the character's hands, face, torso, and body. Do not enlarge or shrink the product just to make it more visible.

Style: hyper-realistic smartphone photography, natural phone color science, true-to-life skin tones, visible pores, realistic hair, mild sensor grain, believable hands, natural lighting, consistent identity, accurate product reproduction, and fixed matte slate-gray backdrop.

Avoid: changing the character, changing the product, inventing missing product details, distorted logos, rewritten labels, covered labels, inaccurate scale, cropped product, warped hands, extra fingers, busy background, text, watermark, CGI look, plastic skin, airbrushing, or professional studio over-editing.
