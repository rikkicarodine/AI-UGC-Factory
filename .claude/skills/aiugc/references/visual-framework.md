# Visual Framework — Vocabulary Vault

Use this before writing any prompt. Make intentional decisions from each relevant category, then translate those decisions into prose. You are a Visual Director — every choice should be deliberate, not improvised.

Different image types demand precision from different elements:
- **UGC / lifestyle** — lives and dies by Subject and Medium. Get skin texture, iPhone camera, and raw energy right.
- **Editorial portrait** — depends on Lighting and Mood. Rim light, atmosphere, color grade.
- **Product shot** — all about Framing and Lighting. Angle, reflection, clean surface.

Read the intent, decide which elements matter most for this shot, and lean into those.

---

## 01 SUBJECT
Everything physically present in the scene — not just who or what, but how it looks. Skin texture, fabric weave, surface imperfections, material grain, micro-details. The more precise the subject description, the more realistic the output.

**Skin & Texture:**
Visible Pores · Bare Skin · Natural Sheen · Fine Baby Hairs · Sweat · Surface Imperfections · Fabric Weave

**Asymmetry & Authenticity (always pick 2–3, subtle):**
Uneven Brows · Asymmetric Eyes · Asymmetric Smile · Subtle Nose Bump · Natural Blemish · Beauty Mark / Mole · Freckle Cluster · Faint Scar · Skin Tone Unevenness · Faint Redness · Under-Eye Shadow · Visible Temple Vein · Slightly Chapped Lips · Imperfect Teeth
*Believable and human, never deforming — the goal is "real person," not a warped face.*

**Expression / Micro-Action:**
Mid-Sentence · Micro-Expression · Engaged Gaze · Mid-Laugh · Looking Away · Eyes Half-Open · Jaw Clenched · Brow Furrowed

**Avoid (Subject):**
No Beauty Mode · No Skin Smoothing · No Airbrushing · No Filters · No Plastic Skin · No Forced Smile · No Stiff Pose

## 02 FRAMING

**Shot Size:**
Extreme Wide · Wide Shot · Medium Wide · Medium Shot · Medium Close-Up · Close-Up · Extreme Close-Up

**Camera Angle:**
Eye Level · Low Angle · High Angle · Bird's Eye · Worm's Eye · Dutch Tilt · Overhead Tilted

**Camera Position / POV:**
First Person POV · Over the Shoulder · Third Person Static · Tracking POV · Surveillance Angle

**Lens Type:**
Ultra Wide 14mm · Ultra Wide 20mm · Wide 24mm · Wide 28mm · Wide 35mm · Standard 50mm · Portrait 85mm · Portrait 105mm · Portrait 135mm · Telephoto 200mm · Telephoto 300mm · Macro

**Focus & Depth:**
Shallow DOF · Deep Focus · Selective Focus · Focus Pull · Tilt-Shift

**Composition Techniques:**
Centered Composition · Rule of Thirds · Golden Ratio · Leading Lines · Framing Within Frame · Symmetry · Asymmetry · Negative Space · Foreground Blur · Layered Depth (FG/MG/BG) · Diagonal Composition · Close Crop

**Avoid (Framing):**
No Lens Distortion · No Vignette · No Over-Cropping · No Dutch Tilt

## 03 LIGHTING

**Light Sources:**

**Lighting Style:**
High Key · Low Key · Rembrandt Lighting · Split Lighting · Butterfly Lighting · Chiaroscuro · Silhouette Lighting · Rim Lighting

**Direction:**
Front Light · Side Light · Back Light · Top Light · Under Light

**Quality:**
Soft Diffused · Hard Direct · Specular · Ambient Fill · Volumetric

**Color:**
Warm · Cool · Mixed Lighting · RGB Lighting · Monochromatic Lighting

**Effects:**
Lens Flare · Light Leaks · Reflections · Wet Surface Reflections · Shadow Patterns · Fog Diffusion · Dust in Light Beams

**Avoid (Lighting):**
No Fill Light · No Blown Highlights · No Flat Lighting · No Harsh Flash · No Overexposure

## 05 MEDIUM

**3D Type:**
