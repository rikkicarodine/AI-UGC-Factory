# Worked Examples — Full UGC Prompts

These are complete prompts that demonstrate the layered framework end to end. They are **inspiration only — never copy, paste, or lightly reskin one.** Use them to absorb the tone, density, and structure, then write a fresh, original prompt tailored to the user's specific request. Every one follows the same arc: opening medium + scene + subject → camera POV → pose → physical identity → skin realism → outfit & accessories → environment → lighting → composition/technical → negative prompt — internalize that arc rather than lifting the wording.

Each example also names the AI generator it was validated against where known (most were generated with GPT Image 2). The structure transfers to Nano Banana 2, Midjourney, and Flux with minor tuning.

**Four rules every example follows:** (1) the shot is either a front-facing selfie or a waist-up photo taken by someone else — never a mirror selfie; (2) this is a *base* model image, so the subject's hands are always free and hold no product or object — lifestyle items (a glass, a cup) may sit in the environment, but never in the hands; (3) the model always faces the camera directly — body squared to the lens, the full front of the figure visible from waist to head, with a direct gaze, never a profile or turned three-quarters away; (4) every model carries 2–3 subtle, real-world imperfections (uneven brows, faint feature asymmetry, a mole or small blemish, tone unevenness) — believable and human, never deforming. Notice how each example below weaves these in rather than describing a flawless face.
